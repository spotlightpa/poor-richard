The <a href="https://www.pa.gov/agencies/pda/food/food-safety/retail-food-inspection-reports">Pennsylvania Department of Agriculture</a> produces retail food inspection reports for 61 of Pennsylvania&#39;s 67 counties using the FDA Model Food Code. Inspections are conducted regularly throughout the state, and results are posted as inspections are conducted. As noted by the Department of Agriculture, inspections are a “snapshot” of a particular day. Many violations are relatively minor and are fixed at the time of inspection.
