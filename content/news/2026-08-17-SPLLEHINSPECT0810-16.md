- <strong>High Priority: </strong>Par cooked chicken breast pieces were held at 110°F (less than one hour), in the hot hold unit at the cook line area, rather than 135°F or above as required. Corrected by reheating to 165°F at the time of this inspection.

- <strong>Low Priority: </strong>Floors under main cookline equipment/ prep tables, walk-in cooler, and dishwashing areas of the food facility are dirty, dusty, and have a build-up of grease, spilled food-debris and in need of cleaning.
