<a href="https://www.spotlightpa.org/restaurant-inspections/#wal-mart-2145-2601-macarthur-rd-whitehall-pa-18052">Wal-Mart \#2145</a><em><br/>2601 Macarthur Rd., Whitehall, PA 18052<br/>Sept. 8, 2026</em>

1 high priority, 3 moderate priority, 4 low priority violations

- <strong>High Priority: </strong>Observed open packages of salmon and shrimp in the cold hold unit with a use by date of 8/18 (Salmon) and 9/7 (Shrimp). Packages were removed and discarded during this inspection.

- <strong>Moderate Priority: </strong>Observed utensils in the bakery handwash sink, indicating uses other than handwashing.

- <strong>Moderate Priority: </strong>Observed 1 gallon 1% milk for sale beyond expiration date. Milk was pulled from the shelf to be disposed of.

- <strong>Moderate Priority: </strong>The Person in Charge did not demonstrate adequate knowledge of food safety in this food facility as evidenced by this non-compliant inspection.

- <strong>Low Priority: </strong>Caulking at wall for the deli 3 compartment sink is deteriorated.

- <strong>Low Priority: </strong>Observed an open employee beverage stored directly on packages of food in the storage area.

- <strong>Low Priority: </strong>Non-food contact surfaces not cleaned at a frequency to preclude accumulation of dirt and soil.<br/>-Accumulation of dust and dirt on the fan guards in the dairy and deli walk in coolers.<br/>-Observed clutter and debris beneath food storage shelving in the walk in coolers.

- <strong>Low Priority: </strong>Observed ice accumulation on packages of food stored in the deli walk in freezer.
