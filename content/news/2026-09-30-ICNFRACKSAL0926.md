It is also generally extremely salty.&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;In 2024, the team took samples from 91 private water wells and springs mostly in Washington County, which is one of the most heavily fracked areas of Pennsylvania. Only six samples found shallow groundwater mixed with brine, and all traces of radium were below regulatory levels.&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;Radium concentrations were higher within three kilometers of oil and gas impoundments and spills, and there was a “weak correlation” between radium activities and the density of oil and gas wells at that distance, the study found. Nathaniel Warner, one of nine researchers who co-authored the paper, said it was difficult to reach conclusions about contamination closer to oil and gas sites because of a lack of samples available within 1 kilometer. &lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;The results showing increased salinity in groundwater are attributable not only to fracking operations but also to road salting and septic tanks, Warner said. But he said that elevated salinity, regardless of the source, was concerning. More salt in water systems can corrode pipes, releasing metals like lead and copper, and it can have a similar effect in aquifers and wells, raising levels of lead, nickel, cobalt, chromium and radium. “It’s like a cascading effect,” Warner said. &lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;Because so many Pennsylvanians rely on private wells for drinking water, and few of those wells are regularly tested, environmentalists have been concerned about contamination caused by oil and gas operations since the beginning of the fracking boom.&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;Critics of fracking have&lt;a href=&#34;https://insideclimatenews.org/news/16042023/pennsylvania-produced-water-fracking-gas/&#34;&gt; long accused&lt;/a&gt; the natural gas industry of causing water contamination near drilling sites. Wastewater associated with drilling may be contaminated with manmade chemicals, or with naturally occurring substances, some of them radioactive, that are brought to the surface when drill bores probe a mile or more underground.&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;In 2025, the &lt;a href=&#34;https://www.epa.gov/hfstudy/questions-and-answers-about-epas-hydraulic-fracturing-drinking-water-assessment#main%20findings&#34;&gt;U.S. Environmental Protection Agency&lt;/a&gt; said fracking can sometimes hurt drinking water supplies as a result of surface spills or underground leaks of chemicals used in the process. Critics of the process often cite the contamination of groundwater at Dimock, in northeastern Pennsylvania, where a resident memorably set his kitchen tap water on fire because of its high concentration of methane, an event recorded in the 2010 movie “Gasland.”&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;Environmentalists worry that spills and leaks of contaminated water may find their way into aquifers that supply drinking water to people living near drilling sites.&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;Even though the study suggests fracking may not be responsible for elevated radium levels around some drilling sites, there is not yet enough evidence to clearly answer the question, Warner said.&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;“There’s not enough overall evidence to conclude that fracking does or does not cause elevated radium,” Warner said. “We have some limited evidence to say that overall, radium is higher. It would be nice to have a lot more samples.”&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;The researchers were looking for signs of higher radium near oil and gas operations, but they found little sign of it, Warner said.&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;“You would expect a certain signature of the radium isotopes and the strontium isotopes.
