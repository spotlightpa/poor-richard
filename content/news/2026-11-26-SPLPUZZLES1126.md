TRIVIA CLUES

1. A lot of Pennsylvanians sat down with their families to eat turkey this week for Thanksgiving. Which <em>other</em> autumn holiday involves eating a different kind of poultry? (Bonus: Where in Pennsylvania is this hyper-regional celebration?)

2. The first Thanksgiving celebrated nationally was proclaimed in which Pennsylvania city? (Hint: It was in 1777.)

3. The first Thanksgiving Day parade took place in Philadelphia in 1920. Which department store was it designed to promote?
