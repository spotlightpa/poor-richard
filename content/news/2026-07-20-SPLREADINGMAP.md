It matters who represents you on Reading City Council.

They help decide what the city spends money on and whether your taxes go up. They’re also your first point of contact in City Hall, helping you get potholes filled or reach the right department to help with neighborhood problems.

But Reading doesn’t make it easy to figure out which <a href="https://www.readingpa.gov/district-maps">district you live in</a> or how to get in touch with your council member.

That’s why Spotlight PA created this interactive, searchable map. Enter your address to find out who your council member is, when they are next up for election, and how to reach them by phone or email.

## What is Reading City Council?

Reading City Council is the legislative body for Reading, Pennsylvania. It is organized into six districts, each represented by a council member elected to serve residents in that specific area.

These members serve alongside the council president, who is elected by voters citywide.

## What do Reading City Council members do?

Reading City Council is responsible for passing ordinances, approving the annual budget, and overseeing city departments.

These duties have a day-to-day impact on residents. Zoning, for example, controls what type of businesses can be in your neighborhood, or how many apartment units can be within a rowhome.

Like in larger forms of government, City Council can act as a check on the executive branch (in this case, the mayor). The mayor’s administration decides the larger priorities for the city, but it’s up to the council to approve mayoral appointments, pass the proposed tax rate, and question spending.

The City Council president represents the city as a whole (rather than a single district) and leads the council meetings.

Many council members also serve as liaisons to city groups that focus on specific issues, such as the Reading Redevelopment Authority, Shade Tree Commission, and Planning Commission.

Council members are considered part-time workers and paid a stipend of $6,250 per year; the council president makes $625 more. They often attend constituent events or private meetings outside of their regular day jobs.

## Can I attend a Reading City Council meeting?

Most council meetings are open to the public and provide a chance for residents to weigh in on issues before votes are taken. Meeting schedules, agendas, and minutes are posted to the <a href="https://www.readingpa.gov/council-meeting-minutes-and-agendas">city’s website at least 24 hours in advance</a>.

The body can meet in private to discuss personnel, legal, or real estate issues.

## How are Reading City Council members elected?

Each member of City Council is elected to four-year terms.

The terms are staggered. Half of the council will be up for election next year, with the remaining seats open in 2029.

Where you live within city limits determines what district you vote in, and each district covers a distinct part of Reading. Boundaries can shift over time as the city&#39;s population changes, meaning district lines you&#39;re familiar with today could change in the future.

If you&#39;ve recently moved, it&#39;s worth checking the map again even if you already knew your district before.
