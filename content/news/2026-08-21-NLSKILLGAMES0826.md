&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/h4&gt;&#10;&lt;p&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&#34;auto&#34;&gt;Normally seeing uniformed state troopers enter a club that has a gambling machine would worry workers or owners of a business that serves alcohol.
