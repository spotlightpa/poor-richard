Sweets are part of Pennsylvania’s DNA.

The Keystone State is home to a number of candy companies and their factories, and you’re probably eating their products this Halloween season. Can you identify them?

PA Local’s Halloween candy quiz asks you to guess which selections from your trick-or-treat bowl — from Twizzlers to Tootsie Rolls — were made right here in Pennsylvania, and which ones weren’t. It has 14 yes-or-no questions, and takes about four minutes to complete.
