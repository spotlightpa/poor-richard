One in four Pennsylvania families are &lt;a href=&#34;https://whyy.org/articles/energy-costs-pennsylvania-renewables/&#34;&gt;struggling to pay&lt;/a&gt; their energy bills, and one in 20 carries significant utility debt. &lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;“Energy has become a kitchen-table issue,” said Elowyn Corby, senior regional director for the Mid-Atlantic at nonprofit Vote Solar.&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;In this environment, community solar is the type of “low-hanging fruit” that should quickly move through the legislature, Saval said. Legislation that &lt;a href=&#34;https://www.palegis.us/legislation/bills/2025/hb504&#34;&gt;passed the House&lt;/a&gt; with support from more than a dozen Republicans would allow Pennsylvanians to subscribe to a local array and cut costs on their electricity bills. Several neighboring states have already opened the door to the practice.&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;Similarly, Democrats and advocates point to battery storage as a sensible solution with a clear path to law. A proposal to &lt;a href=&#34;https://www.palegis.us/legislation/bills/2025/hb1260&#34;&gt;make warehouses solar-ready&lt;/a&gt; has also sat in the Senate after clearing the House and could complement other actions on energy policy.&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;Rep. This bill could be a “starting point,” Saval said. Pennsylvania is among a small number of states that lack a &lt;a href=&#34;https://app.publicnewsservice.org/story/growing-push-to-advance-statewide-energy-siting-reform-in-pa-1/65525694-b647-4938-b1bd-dda0d6c0f556?&#34;&gt;statewide energy siting policy&lt;/a&gt;, making it a target for reform. &lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;“It would lower our energy bills in noticeable ways,” said Alex Bomstein, executive director of nonprofit Clean Air Council. “It would make our air cleaner. Legislators and advocates said a House-approved proposal to &lt;a href=&#34;https://trackdatacenters.com/usa/pennsylvania&#34;&gt;regulate data centers&lt;/a&gt; and limit their impact on electricity bills could pass in a Democratic trifecta. &lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;The environmental concerns around data centers go well beyond electricity. Nearly three-quarters said they would &lt;a href=&#34;https://poll.qu.edu/poll-release?releaseid=3962&#34;&gt;oppose having one built&lt;/a&gt; in their community, according to a recent Quinnipiac poll. The stiff public opposition has given rise to calls for a moratorium on new data center construction.&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;“We should push pause until and unless the industry can show that their negative effects on the environment and public health and our pocketbooks can get resolved,” Masur said. &lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;And yet legislators and advocates said a moratorium is unlikely, given Shapiro’s &lt;a href=&#34;https://insideclimatenews.org/news/06022026/pennsylvania-governor-josh-shapiro-data-center-growth-consumer-protections/&#34;&gt;extensive courtship of the industry&lt;/a&gt;—even despite his recent executive order that &lt;a href=&#34;https://www.inquirer.com/politics/pennsylvania/josh-shapiro-data-center-order-20260818.html&#34;&gt;requires local approval&lt;/a&gt; for projects to receive state permits. &lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;“He is the limiting factor,” said Sen. Shapiro’s proposed energy reforms include the Pennsylvania Reliable Energy Sustainability Standard (PRESS), which would add nuclear power, fusion and “clean forms of natural gas” to the state’s alternative energy portfolio, and the Pennsylvania Climate Emissions Reduction Act (PACER), a state-specific cap-and-trade program.&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;But assuming Democrats hold narrow margins in the House and Senate, getting much done without upsetting key factions—from labor unions to fossil fuel-friendly legislators—won’t be easy. He backed out of the agreement to cut carbon emissions from power plants in a budget deal that critics said sacrificed climate action to restore normal state funding.  &lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;“Pennsylvanians are not just eager for progress but also rightfully demanding it,” Corby said.&lt;/p&gt;&#10;&#10;&#10;&#10;&lt;p class=&#34;wp-block-paragraph&#34;&gt;Although legislation has consistently stalled in the Senate in recent sessions, the House has developed “a very compelling narrative of preparation,” Corby said, paving the way for a trifecta to move bills with far-reaching potential on energy, climate and the environment.
